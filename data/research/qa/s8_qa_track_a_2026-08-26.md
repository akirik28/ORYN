homepage link rather than a specific, decision-grade opportunity (no deadline, no specific course,
no curated value proposition) — see Structural Findings §5.

## Structural findings (recurring 2+ times, not individual-row issues)

**1. Dry-run "awaiting approval" documents go stale the moment a write actually happens, with no
signal that it happened.** Both `GAP_CLOSURE_5RECORD_DRYRUN_2026-08-24.md` and
`TUBITAK_6OLYMPIAD_DRYRUN_2026-08-24.md` state in their own closing lines that nothing has been
written to production ("No production write yet," "Awaiting review before write"). Live SQL
confirms all 11 proposed records were in fact written, `created_at` timestamped 21:50–22:04 UTC on
2026-08-23 — hours before either document's own file-save time. A reader following only the
document (as this audit initially did, before checking the DB directly) would reasonably but
wrongly conclude nothing had been applied. Recommend every dry-run doc receive a one-line
"APPLIED [timestamp] by [session]" stamp the moment a coordinating session executes it.

**2. Stale "still open" institutional banners are a systemic, cross-institution pattern — and our
own defense catches most instances but not all.** Found independently in this pass on **four**
separate Turkish university micro-sites: İTÜ (`cycle_status` wrongly `upcoming` for a 41-day-past
deadline), Özyeğin's HSRI research program (`cycle_status='closed'` self-contradicted by its own
description text, both stale relative to the live source), Sabancı (honestly hedged, not a
violation), and Istanbul Bilgi University (14+ months stale, honestly hedged in free text but not
in the structured `deadline` field). This is the same failure mode CR1 already named three times
elsewhere (Özyeğin's sibling Summer School row, env-olympiad.com, an earlier IPsyO false start).
Where the pipeline sets `cycle_status` to `closed`/`historical` with a specific plain-language
note — confirmed correct on roughly 30 of the ~40 past-deadline rows swept in this pass — this is
handled well and matches AGENTS.md's data-freshness intent. It fails specifically when a stale
institutional banner is trusted at face value without cross-checking today's date against the
stored deadline. Recommend a scheduled re-sweep keyed to `deadline < current_date AND cycle_status
NOT IN ('closed','historical')`, which would have caught İTÜ immediately.

**3. A checkable, non-zero fee is being stored as `NULL`, and it recurs specifically on rows
where payment is a stated mandatory step.** Three rows in this pass alone: JEI (fee confirmed as
mandatory "step 1" of submission, `cost` still `null` three days after being flagged with a
specific $49 figure), THIMUN (a fully quotable €340+/person fee, never captured), and InvestIN
(strong circumstantial evidence of a paid model via its own "Scholarship Scheme," exact price not
captured). This is the same shape the CR1 handoff already flagged for JRHS/IJHSR vs. JEI ("the
data gap runs systematically in favour of the expensive option") — and it is recurring on fresh
rows, not just the original one. `NULL` is technically honest ("unknown") but functionally
indistinguishable from "free" to any UI or matching logic that doesn't parse free-text
descriptions. Recommend a scripted sweep for rows where `cost IS NULL` but the description
contains fee-indicating language ("fee," "$," "€," "payment," "tuition").

**4. Positive, counterbalancing finding: the harder eligibility judgment calls are landing
correctly far more often than the raw defect count might suggest.** Breakthrough Junior Challenge
(dual-date age math, sanctions-based exclusion framing, English-language requirement), Wharton
(team size + mandatory teacher advisor), Coca-Cola Scholars (precise US-persons-only citizenship
language), and TechGirls (a specific 37-country annual list including Türkiye, quoted exactly) all
matched their live official sources on every field checked. The entire TÜBİTAK/gap-closure batch
(11 new records) exists correctly gated. The defect surface actually found in this audit clusters
narrowly around two identifiable failure classes — stale Turkish-institution cycle banners and
missing fee amounts — rather than being spread evenly across citizenship/age/language rules, which
are the categories the product's non-negotiable requirements (no false admissions precision, no
invented eligibility) care about most.

**5. A small number of rows are generic commercial-platform links rather than decision-grade
opportunities.** Coursera is the clearest example: no deadline, no specific course, no curated
value proposition, just a homepage link with a two-sentence description. Nothing on the row is
factually wrong, but it doesn't fit the "decision-grade coverage over row count" bar the
opportunity engine is supposed to hold (per prior project direction). Worth a curation pass to
either attach a specific, dated program or retire rows of this shape — this is a design/curation
question for the owning lane, not a QA rejection on its own.

## Records with a real issue worth a note

### #8 — Northwestern CTD 3-Week Academic Summer Camps (S5A-0028) — VERIFIED, citation/URL mismatch

The record's `evidence` array attributes this quote to `https://www.ctd.northwestern.edu/eligibility`:
> "Students from across the United States and internationally have participated in CTD
> programs...International students can apply to Northwestern CTD online programs. Residential
> programs on the Northwestern campus may have additional requirements for international
> participants, including visa considerations."

I fetched `/eligibility` twice this session — once normally, once asking for full verbatim page
text — and this quote does not appear anywhere on that page (which is about testing tiers: Amber/
Emerald/Magenta/Indigo, not international access). A WebSearch located the actual source: a
different official CTD page, `https://ctd.northwestern.edu/ctd-international-students`
("International Student Information"), which I fetched directly and confirms the substance closely
("Each year CTD hosts students from around the world in its online and in-person programs,"
plus an explicit B-1/B-2 visa requirement for residential attendance). So **the underlying claim is
true and well-supported — just cited to the wrong URL**. The record's other citation, the ASSET
Talent Search partner-country list (India, UAE, Kuwait, Singapore — not Turkey), checked out exactly
on `ctd.northwestern.edu/asset-talent-search`. Net: the `ELIGIBLE_WITH_CONDITIONS` conclusion holds;
recommend swapping the source URL for that one evidence line before this is treated as fully
first-party-clean.

### #16 — Weizmann Institute Alpha Program — VERIFIED, corroborated but not first-party this session

This is a prose-only rejection in S5B's handoff (not a formal JSONL record — S5B's own methodology
correctly excludes confirmed-ineligible finds from the candidate files). The primary source
(`davidson.weizmann.ac.il/en/programs/alpha-program`) 301-redirects to `davidson.org.il`, and every
path I tried on the new domain 404'd this session — a genuine site-reachability issue, not a
citation problem. I corroborated via the closely-affiliated "Future Scientists Center"
(`madaney.net`), explicitly named in the program's own description as its co-launching partner: its
page confirms the program is funded jointly with "the Ministry of Education – the Department for
Talented and Gifted Students," uses Israel's own grade-numbering system (י'-י"א, i.e. 10th-11th
grade), and its application documentation references "certificate for new immigrant/returning
resident" — status categories that only make sense for applicants already inside Israel's civil/
immigration system. This is consistent with, and supports, the handoff's own careful framing
("Israeli national gifted-education pathway via a national testing institute, not internationally
accessible" — correctly a *structural* pathway claim, not an overclaimed blanket citizenship bar,
mirroring the same distinction S5A drew for American Legion Boys State). I could not get a first-
party quote directly off Weizmann's own site this session due to the dead links, so this is
corroborated-but-not-primary-sourced rather than fully closed.

### #10 — ODTÜ/METU — record itself is accurate; handoff's own summary table miscounts it

Not a defect in the record (S5A-0031), which is fully confirmed: 60,000 TL 2025 fee, 30 Haziran–11
Temmuz 2025 dates, and the 2026→Radyo ODTÜ organizer-transition sentence all matched
`metusummerschool.org` verbatim, and no citizenship restriction was found (matching the record's
claim). The issue is in the **handoff's own STATUS section**: it lists ODTÜ/METU as one of "4
`ELIGIBLE_WITH_CONDITIONS`" records ("carries a cost/organizer caveat but the eligibility question
itself is clean"). But the record's actual `turkey_student_access` field value is
`VERIFIED_ELIGIBLE`, not `ELIGIBLE_WITH_CONDITIONS` — and the record's own reasoning text ("the
eligibility question itself is straightforward") is internally consistent with `VERIFIED_ELIGIBLE`,
not with the conditions bucket. Re-running the count across all 28 `PRODUCTION_READY` S5A records
confirms: actual breakdown is **25 `VERIFIED_ELIGIBLE` / 3 `ELIGIBLE_WITH_CONDITIONS`** (Telluride
TASS, Northwestern CTD, Emory Pre-College only), not the handoff's stated "24 / 4." Cosmetic —
doesn't change any record's status_recommendation or the 28-count total — but worth fixing in the
handoff text so CEO/DATA's read of the conditional-cases list is accurate.

## What this sample does and doesn't cover

This was 20 of 38+31 records (roughly 29%), weighted toward the highest-risk categories per the
brief: every `ELIGIBLE_WITH_CONDITIONS` case in both lanes' priority lists, both net-new discoveries
in S5A, both free/nonprofit priority picks in S5B, both formal rejections plus the one prose-only
rejection, the one self-flagged anomaly, and half of S5B's already-live gap-closure corrections. It
is not a claim that the other ~49 records are clean — S5A's own ~35-row remaining gap-closure backlog
and S5B's 13 `NEEDS_REVIEW` candidates are explicitly still open per both handoffs. Within this
sample, both lanes' evidence discipline held up well under independent re-fetching: every quote I
could locate at all matched the source closely, the two "gaps" found were citation/URL-attribution
issues rather than fabrications, and both lanes' own self-imposed caution (Istanbul Bilgi's
`BLOCKED_UNCLEAR`, SSP/HARP's medium-confidence flags, Zooniverse's age-policy gap) proved to be
accurate self-assessment rather than either overclaiming or excessive hedging.

## KEY FINDINGS

- **Nordic/Baltic/Greek government bachelor's scholarships are essentially a dead end for this product**, and this is itself a useful, decision-grade finding, not just an absence of results. The pattern is consistent enough to state with confidence: none of Greece, Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, or Iceland has a general-purpose, standalone, undergraduate-level government scholarship that a Turkey-based high-school senior could apply to as a distinct action. Two genuine "traps" were found matching the pattern the brief warned about: the Norwegian Quota Scheme is dead (ended 2016) but still listed as live on aggregator sites, and Denmark/Finland's "scholarships" are not separately-applied-for at all (automatically bundled into ordinary university admission — the same disqualifying pattern Wave 1 used for Reach Oxford). This confirms Wave 1's existing accepted set (Stipendium Hungaricum, NL Scholarship, MEXT, Taiwan, GKS) is not an artifact of incomplete search — those really are the standout options in this category.
- **The Global Korea Scholarship Türkiye-quota question is now definitively resolved**: Turkey has a confirmed, named, non-zero quota (2 scholars) in the official 2026 Embassy Track country table. This was the single cleanest resolution in this wave and should be upgraded with confidence.
- **Turkish-American diaspora organizations mostly cannot help a Turkey-based (not diaspora) applicant fund NEW study abroad.** The Üçok Family Scholarship Fund is the one clean exception found — worth spotlighting in-product given its size (up to $50k/year, 4 years) and genuine Türkiye-citizen accessibility, though its far-future next cycle (Fall 2027) and gender restriction should be represented honestly.

A human with a real browser, or a different fetch tool/IP, is the more promising path if this needs to reach VERIFIED.

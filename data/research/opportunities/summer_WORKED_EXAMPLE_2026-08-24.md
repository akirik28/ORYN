## What it could say, from tonight's verified facts

### Do this in the next three weeks

**1. Ask your school to nominate you for HKUST I·ELITE.** *"Membership Fee **Free**."* The nomination window is
***"Sep 11 – Nov 20, 2026"*** — it opens in under three weeks. Two real gates: *"by **School Nomination
Only!**"* and *"Ranked in the **top 20% of the grade**"*. Eligibility is written across four school systems —
*"Year 11 / Grade 10 / Form 4 / 高一 or above"*. **The action is not "apply" — it is "talk to your school",**
because the nomination has to come from them, and that takes lead time.

**2. Start Schoolhouse.world now.** Free, minimum age 13, no cycle — available today, from Türkiye. The gate is
real work, not a form: *"Achieve at least a 90% on the unit test **while explaining your reasoning aloud**"* on
a recorded video, then *"peer review two videos from students around the world."* And **MIT Admissions' own
page** says: ***"MIT also accepts calculus certifications through Schoolhouse.world."*** For a student whose
school offers no calculus, this is the single highest-value free thing in the entire corpus.

**3. Find an AP test centre before mid-November.** Your school not offering AP does not exclude you. College
Board: *"**Yes.** You can't order AP Exams directly, but you should be able to arrange to take exams at a
nearby high school that administers AP Exams."* Search the AP Course Ledger, then contact that school's AP
coordinator. ***"The deadline for schools to submit AP Exam orders is mid-November."*** ⚠️ *Whether authorised
international test centres exist for students with no local AP school is **not established** — that is the
first thing to check.*

### Prepare now, apply in winter

**4. SUMaC Online — a selective Stanford mathematics programme you can attend from Türkiye.** Stanford's own
words: *"The online experience and residential programs have **equal levels of academic rigor and content**."*
**64 online places** (vs 40 residential). ⚠️ Check the clock before anything else: sessions run
*"8:00am-11:00am **or** 5:00pm-8:00pm **PT**"* — from Türkiye the first is early evening and the second is the
middle of the night.

**5. Canada/USA Mathcamp — merit-gated, no application fee, no TOEFL.** *"Application materials for our 2026
summer program will be available **January 12**"*, deadline *"**February 23, 2026**"*. Selection is by a
**Qualifying Quiz**. Ages **13–18**. *"**There is no application fee.**"* And: *"We do not require the TOEFL
for non-native speakers, **but** our program is conducted entirely in English, so you should only apply if you
are completely comfortable interacting mathematically and socially in English."* **Not rolling** — *"all
applications received by the deadline will receive equal consideration"* — so applying early buys nothing.

**6. PROMYS → but the European door — and it's open to you specifically, confirmed.** *"Pre-university
students who are 16 or older and **ordinarily resident in Europe** are eligible to apply to **PROMYS
Europe** at the University of Oxford."* Applications open in **January**. Entrance **problem set**;
*"Generous need-based financial aid… up to the full cost."* **Updated 2026-08-24, no longer a
question mark:** PROMYS Europe's own 2026 page defines its own term — *"we include in this all
countries adjacent to the Mediterranean"* — and Türkiye has a Mediterranean coastline. Covered on a
plain reading of the provider's own words, not an inference. This is now a real, actionable door, not
a maybe.

**7. TASS — free, elite, and open to you, with an asymmetry to plan around.** *"TASS is completely free — We
cover all of the program costs—tuition, books, room and board, and field trips."* Expect the 2027 round to open
**~15 October 2026**. ⚠️ *"International students… must be **sophomores** to apply"* — where US students may
apply as sophomores **or** juniors. **If you are a junior, this door is already shut; if you are a sophomore,
it is one of the best offers in the catalogue.**

### Do not spend money on these expecting selectivity

- **Oxford Royale** — £6,995 residential. Its own page: *"…but does **not operate under the aegis of the
  University of Oxford**."* No admission mechanism of any kind is stated.
- **Cambridge Scholars' Programme** — its footer: *"The company is founded and run by Cambridge graduates,
  hires Cambridge students and academics who act in a personal capacity, **but does not operate under the aegis
  of the University of Cambridge**."* Its "application" form collects name, email, and Student/Parent.
- **Summer Discovery** — $2,499–$16,999, universities named only as *locations*, no admission criteria stated.
- **Oxford Scholastica** — £7,495, *"limited number of places which tend to become **fully booked early in the
  year**"* — scarcity by booking speed, not merit; £995 taken **before** the decision.

### Doors that are closed to you — so you don't waste the effort

**MITES**, **Clark Scholars** and **Stanford SIMR** are free or paid, and excellent, and **US citizens or
permanent residents only**. **Caltech SRC** is limited to *one Californian school district*. **Harvard CURE**
— free, prestigious, a real Dana-Farber cancer-research pipeline — is narrower still: *"residing in or
attending school in **Massachusetts**"*, a single US state, not even citizenship-gated the way the others are.
**Sutton Trust** is written in UK school-system terms (*Year 12 / S5 / GCSEs*). **LSE Summer School** requires
*"an **offer of a place at university**"*. None of this is in ORYN today.

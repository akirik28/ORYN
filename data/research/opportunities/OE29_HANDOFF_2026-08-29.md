## Why this scope, not summer schools

Before researching anything, this lane searched the repo first, per instruction. Two
findings shaped everything below:

1. **Competitions and genuine research already have a large, high-quality, unapplied
   dry-run corpus** — `data/research/opportunities/cr1_*` (2026-08-23, ~100 records: TÜBİTAK
   2202/2204-A, CERN BL4S, CrowdMath, Regeneron STS/ISEF, the IMO-family olympiads, SJWP,
   Davidson Fellows, IJSO, and more) plus `leadership_batch{1..5}_2026-08-21.jsonl` (25
   civic/leadership/entrepreneurship records) and `thincat_batch{1..4}_2026-08-21.jsonl`
   (16 records across research/internship/scholarship/fellowship/volunteering). Re-researching
   any of this would have been waste, not value.
2. **Economics/business/entrepreneurship and internships were genuinely thin** — existing
   coverage totaled roughly 10-15 lines across all prior files combined, exactly matching
   the founder's own emphasis. This lane's effort went there, plus a handful of named
   competition leads (GENIUS Olympiad, FIRST-family, Waterloo/CEMC, and specific
   writing/humanities gaps) explicitly called out as leads to verify.

## Strongest newly discovered opportunities

1. **World Robot Olympiad** (OED-006) — 95+ countries, and Türkiye's national chapter
   (Bilim Kahramanları Derneği) literally hosted the 2024 international final in İzmir.
   The single strongest new find in the whole batch.
2. **Economics for Leaders** (OEA-004, Foundation for Teaching Economics) — genuinely
   selective (~2 applicants/spot, stated on the org's own site), worldwide-admission
   summer program on real university campuses (Yale, Berkeley, Cornell, Duke, Penn), with
   the honest caveat that international students get no financial aid.
3. **TechGirls** (OEC-001, U.S. Department of State) — fully funded (visa, airfare,
   housing, insurance), genuinely competitive, and Türkiye is explicitly on the official
   2026 eligible-country list. The one internship-category record that is both
   merit-selected and confirmed Turkey-accessible.
4. **Verge Challenge** (OEB-001) — official page states outright "Open to students from
   any country. Location has no effect on how your submission is scored," $10k/$5k/$2.5k
   prizes, currently open.
5. **Concours Général des Lycées — SES** (OEA — researched by the France-adjacent pass,
   see notes) and **Bebras/Bilge Kunduz** (OED-010) — both are the cleanest examples found
   of a structural access barrier that is NOT nationality-based: gated by school type
   (French-curriculum lycées; a Turkish national chapter) rather than citizenship, exactly
   the distinction the brief asked to capture precisely.

## Biggest remaining coverage gaps

- **Genuinely selective AND internationally-accessible corporate internships are close to
  nonexistent for this age band.** Every well-documented merit-based program found (EY
  Foundation, Deloitte, Cristo Rey) turned out to be geographically/structurally gated
  (UK-state-school-plus-benefits, or US-enrollment-plus-work-authorization). This reads as
  a real structural scarcity, not a research shortfall — confirmed independently by two
  different research passes reaching the same conclusion from different angles.
- **No investment/portfolio competition beyond Wharton/YIS was found that is both merit-judged
  and internationally open.** A real, structural gap.
- **FIRST Tech Challenge and FIRST LEGO League's Turkish access rest on secondary evidence
  only** — the presumed operator (Fikret Yüksel Foundation) blocked direct fetch (HTTP 403)
  on every attempt. FLL also has a hard sunset: FIRST and LEGO Education's partnership ends
  after the 2026-27 season.
- **An unresolved internal data-quality question surfaced in passing, not by this lane's
  own design**: `docs/research/opportunities-competitions-s6/EXISTING_COMPETITION_BASELINE.md`
  and `data/research/registry/SESSION_CLOSEOUT_2026-08-26_to_27.md` contain contradictory
  statements about whether a duplicate FRC row was actually merged on 2026-08-26/27. A
  direct live-DB check was attempted to resolve this and was correctly blocked by the
  permission system (this lane is research-only) — flagging for whoever owns that merge.

## Source-quality concerns

- **WebFetch reliability was poor for several official-but-dynamic sites this session**:
  federalreserveeducation.org, vexrobotics.com, recf.org, fikretyukselfoundation.org, and
  three Turkish Bebras domains all returned 403/TLS/connection errors on direct fetch
  despite being real, live, findable sites. Every record affected by this is marked
  PARTIALLY_VERIFIED rather than VERIFIED, with the specific blocked domain named.
- **Two self-caught deadline-cycle contradictions**, corrected before finalizing rather than
  silently resolved one way: National Personal Finance Challenge's "now open" registration
  language alongside an already-past National Finals date (most likely a stale leftover
  reference, not re-guessed); National High School Fed Challenge's 2025-2026 dates
  presented as historical rather than upcoming.
- **One clean example of a domain that looked right but wasn't**: a generic
  "science-fair.org" URL surfaced during I-SWEEEP research turned out, on fetch, to belong
  to an unrelated regional fair (SCVSEFA) — not used as evidence, recorded as a caution for
  future passes.
- **Two agents in this batch stalled/lost their session state during a severe, unrelated
  local-disk-full incident mid-sprint** (disk hit absolute zero, then stabilized under
  200MB free). One completion notification (economics/finance, first attempt) could not be
  reconciled with its own resumed instance's memory on retry — treated as a probable
  state-persistence casualty of the disk incident, not fabricated, and redone from scratch
  rather than reconstructed from a summary. See the parent session's own record of this
  incident for detail; not repeated here since it's infrastructure, not research.

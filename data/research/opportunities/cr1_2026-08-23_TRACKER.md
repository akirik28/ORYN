## What "decision-grade" means here

Derived from `evaluateRecommendationReadiness()` in `lib/opportunities/readiness.ts`, the gate the
product actually enforces — not a parallel definition. Hard blockers there: missing title/organizer,
no traceable URL, `verification_state != 'verified_current'`, non-actionable `cycle_status`.
Eligibility/deadline/cost absences are quality signals, never blockers (spec: unknown stays unknown).

- **A — decision-grade**: passes every hard blocker AND carries deadline (or an explicit official
  "not announced"), age/grade, country/residency eligibility, cost, and a working application route.
- **B — high-value, missing 1–2 critical facts.**
- **C — weak / commercial / low-value.** Lead-gen contests, pay-to-publish venues.
- **D — exclude / hold / wrong corpus.**

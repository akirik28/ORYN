# What a student can actually act on — autumn 2026

Built from dates **verified against official pages** during this lane's overnight run, spanning
2026-08-23 into 2026-08-24 — most rows below were captured on the earlier date and not re-timestamped
individually; where this file has since been directly refreshed against a later finding, that's noted
inline. This is the "what should I do next?" answer for a student opening ORYN in late August, and it
is the thing the catalogue currently cannot produce: of 252 summer rows, **26 carried a deadline** at
last count and `application_open_date` is empty on every row where it would matter.

**Why open dates matter more than deadlines right now.** In late August almost every summer programme is
*closed*. A deadline five months away is not an action; an **opening date** three weeks away is. ORYN has the
column (`application_open_date`, migration 0041) and does not use it.

## A pattern worth surfacing on its own: the financial-aid deadline is earlier than the application deadline

Seen three times tonight, always as a *separate, earlier* date — and it is the one that matters most to the
students least able to pay:

- **Harvard SSP** — *"Early Application & **Priority Financial Aid** Deadline: Wednesday, January 7, 2026"*, a
  full five weeks before the regular deadline of *"February 11, 2026"*.
- **Rutgers Young Scholars** — *"Applications for **financial assistance** must be received by **April 15,
  2026**"*, separate from the main application process.
- **PROMYS** — *"To be considered for financial aid, please have a parent or guardian complete the financial
  aid form by **March 15**."*
- **University of St Andrews** — *"Scholarship applications close on the **25 February 2026**"*, and the
  scholarships are large: one **full** £6,850 award, two half awards of £3,425, six partial awards of £1,141.
- **SUISS (Edinburgh)** — *"Scholarship application deadline: **27 March 2026**"*, four weeks before the general
  *"Application deadline: 24 April 2026"*.

ORYN stores one `deadline` per row. In each of these it would store the *later*, general one — and a student
who needs aid would miss the date that actually governs them. **Five programmes, five separate aid deadlines,
and in every case the aid date is the earlier one.** This is not a coincidence of scheduling: funded places are
allocated before general admission closes, so the students with the least money face the tightest deadline.

## The free programmes — and which of them an international student can actually enter

Cost is the largest barrier in this corpus (rows run to $15,200). Everything below is **verified free** from an
official page. The split is the point.

**The pattern, stated plainly:** *the most prestigious free programmes in this catalogue are the ones ORYN's
users cannot enter.* Seven of the eight free US programmes above carry a citizenship, district, state-residency,
or need-document wall — several (Caltech SRC, CU Boulder PCDP, Harvard CURE) are narrower than a citizenship
bar, excluding almost all US students too. **Most of those walls are not recorded in ORYN today** (Harvard
CURE's residency_restrictions field was, on checking directly — the other gaps stand). The genuinely open free
options are a much shorter list — and two of the best of them (HKUST, Kadir Has) currently sit in the corpus
with `cost = NULL`, no dates and no tier.

## What this calendar shows about the product

Almost every dated item above came from a page ORYN already links to — the dates were simply never captured.
The gap is not discovery; it is that `application_open_date` is unused and `deadline` is filled on 26 of 252
rows. **In late August, a catalogue that only stores deadlines has almost nothing to say.**

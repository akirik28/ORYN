# TÜBİTAK 2204-A — the university-admission rights, article by article

Lane RES-CR1 · retrieved 2026-08-23 · source: TÜBİTAK BİDEB **2026 Çağrı Duyurusu, 2204-A**
(`2204_A_cagri_metni_2025-2026_08.10.2025.pdf`, 17pp), extracted locally with `pypdf` because
WebFetch could not parse the PDF. Quotes are verbatim Turkish from that document.

> **Why this matters:** these are not profile decoration. They are mechanisms that change how a
> student is placed into a Turkish university. **They are also two different rights with different
> qualifying conditions**, and a record that merges them would be wrongly permissive — it would tell
> a national winner they have a right they do not have.

## The two rights are NOT the same

| | **§7.1.1 — Ek Katsayı** (extra coefficient) | **§7.1.2 — Sınavsız yerleştirme** (placement without the exam) |
|---|---|---|
| **Who qualifies** | national final **1st/2nd/3rd**, **OR** top-three at an international competition **TÜBİTAK sent them to** | **ONLY** top-three at an international competition **TÜBİTAK sent them to** |
| **National winner alone qualifies?** | **YES** | **NO** |
| **What you get** | a coefficient uplift **proportional to the placing** | placed by ÖSYM **without sitting the exam** |
| **Field constraint** | must choose a department related to the **ana alan** they placed in | must choose a listed programme related to the ana alan they placed in **at the final** |
| **When** | **first year** they sit the university exam, **once only** | **first year** they sit the exam |
| **Separate application?** | **YES** — to TÜBİTAK, within the window in the current YKS guide | **YES** — same |

**The distinction in one line:** placing top-three nationally earns you an *uplift*. Being sent
abroad by TÜBİTAK and placing top-three there is what earns *exam-free placement*.

## Three conditions that can silently void the benefit

1. **It is not automatic.** Both rights require a **separate application to TÜBİTAK**, inside a
   window defined by that year's YKS guide. The document puts the burden explicitly on the student:
   *"Ek katsayı başvuru sürecinin takibi proje sahibi öğrencilerin sorumluluğundadır."* A student
   who wins and simply waits gets nothing.
2. **First exam year only, once only.** *"üniversite sınavına girecekleri ilk yıl, bir defaya mahsus
   olmak üzere."* A student who sits YKS, is unhappy, and re-sits the next year has spent the right.
3. **Field-locked.** The benefit only applies if the chosen department relates to the **ana alan**
   the placing was won in. Winning in Biology does not help an application to Law.

## Year-dependency — flagged, not resolved

The call document points to the **2025 YKS Kılavuzu** for the detail of both rights. These
entitlements are defined by ÖSYM's guide for a given year and **can change year to year**. So:

- The *existence* of the two rights is P1-solid from TÜBİTAK's own 2026 call document.
- The *precise magnitude* of the ek katsayı, and the exact list of eligible programmes for sınavsız
  yerleştirme, live in the ÖSYM YKS guide and are **not verified here**.
- **Do not state a numeric coefficient anywhere.** I did not retrieve one and will not infer one.

## Also in the same section — scholarship access (§7.2)

- **§7.2.1** national final top-three get advantages when applying to **2205 Lisans Burs Programı**,
  **2247-C Stajyer Araştırmacı Burs Programı (Genç STAR)**, **2224-D Yurt Dışındaki Bilimsel Eğitim
  Etkinliklerine Katılımı Destekleme Programı**. Their advisors get access to 2247-C (STAR Öğretmen).
- **§7.2.2** students sent abroad by TÜBİTAK who win 1st–**4th** place **or a special prize** get
  advantages for **2205**, **2213-A Yurt Dışı Doktora Burs Programı**, **2224-D**. Note this tier
  reaches **further down** than §7.1 — 4th place and special prizes count here but not for the
  admission rights.
- **§2.2.6** students going to international competitions receive **project development support and
  academic advisor support** from TÜBİTAK.

## Entry conditions worth carrying into the record (same document)

- **§4.1.1** *"Yarışmaya, Türkiye ve KKTC'de örgün ve **açık öğretim** sisteminde öğrenim gören tüm
  lise öğrencileri katılabilir."* — open-education students included. Criterion is **where you
  study**, not citizenship.
- **§4.1.6** must not have turned 20 as of the closing date.
- **§4.1.2** one project per student; max three students per project.
- **§4.1.7** the project must be **finished** — *"Fikir aşamasında veya devam etmekte olan
  projelerle yarışmaya başvuru yapılmaz."*
- **§4.1.9 exclusivity** — a project found to have been submitted to **any other** competition before
  this one's closing date is disqualified at any stage. **§4.1.8** bars re-entering a previous year's
  project. Together these make sequencing a real strategic constraint, and the advisor must raise it
  *before* a student double-submits.
- **§3** *"Yarışma için yılda bir kez başvuru alınır. Çağrı açılış ve kapanış tarihleri… yarışmanın
  web sayfasında ilan edilir."* — once a year, dates published separately. **Explicitly not rolling.**
- **Cost: no fee appears anywhere in the 17-page call.** §2.1.2 TÜBİTAK covers travel, accommodation
  and meals for students invited to the regional and final stages, plus one advisor/teacher.

## CLOSED 2026-08-23: which international competition, and a fourth stage nobody had

When this document was first written, its biggest open question was: **§7.1.2 turns on being "sent by
TÜBİTAK to an international competition" — but which competitions?** The call document never names one.

**It is now answered, from TÜBİTAK's own newsroom.** On its EUCYS page TÜBİTAK writes:

> "Ülkemiz adına yarışmaya katılan projeler ise, TÜBİTAK tarafından 56 yıldır düzenlenen **Lise
> Öğrencileri Araştırma Projeleri Yarışması** sonucunda belirleniyor. Projeler, ön değerlendirme,
> bölge değerlendirmesi, final değerlendirmesi ve **uluslararası yarışmalara katılacakların
> seçilmesi** olmak üzere **dört aşamadan** geçerek seçiliyor."

Two things follow.

**1. EUCYS is one of the international competitions §7.1.2 refers to.** The European Union Contest
for Young Scientists — ages 14-20, teams up to 3, entry only by national-contest first-prize
nomination, prizes to €7,000. The Commission's own pages independently record Türkiye winning there.

**2. There is a FOURTH stage, and it changes the advice.** This document (and the call) described
three: preliminary → regional → final. TÜBİTAK names a fourth — **selection of who goes to
international competitions** — after the national final.

That matters because the two rights split exactly across it:

- **Ek katsayı (§7.1.1)** — reachable at the **national final**. Placing top-three there is enough.
- **Sınavsız yerleştirme (§7.1.2)** — requires being **sent abroad and placing top-three there**.
  Winning nationally does not put you on the plane; the fourth stage does.

So the honest framing for a student is: the national final earns you a coefficient uplift and makes
you *eligible to be considered* for the international stage. Exam-free placement is two selections
away, not one.

**EUCYS is also a hub, not a terminus.** TÜBİTAK records that its 2025 second prize carried €5,000
**plus fully-funded places at the London International Youth Science Forum and the Stockholm
International Youth Science Forum**, and that a special prize carried a funded trip to the University
of Oxford. Placing at EUCYS opens further international opportunities.

**Still not established:** whether 2204-A is also Türkiye's route to **Regeneron ISEF**. TÜBİTAK
writes "uluslararası yarışmalar" in the plural, so EUCYS is confirmed as *one* destination, not the
only one. Do not tell a student that 2204-A leads to ISEF.

*(TÜBİTAK's page names the three prize-winning students. Their names are deliberately not recorded in
any ORYN artifact — they are school students, the names carry no product value, and AGENTS.md §12
directs minimising data on minors.)*

## Unresolved

- **Next cycle dates.** The 2026 call ran 2025-10-13 → 2025-12-12 17:30. A 2027 cycle would be
  expected to open around **October 2026 — within weeks of this writing** — but is unannounced.
  Re-check the competition page in October; do not infer.
- **Prize amounts** are published on the competition web page, not in the call document.
- **Which international competitions TÜBİTAK sends winners to.** §7.1.2 turns on this and the
  document never names them. Whether Regeneron ISEF is among them — and whether TÜBİTAK is Türkiye's
  ISEF-affiliated fair — is **unverified**. It matters: if it is, 2204-A is simultaneously the
  Turkish route to ISEF *and* to exam-free placement.
- The **magnitude** of the ek katsayı (ÖSYM YKS guide, not retrieved).

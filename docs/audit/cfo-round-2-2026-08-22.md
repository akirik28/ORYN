## Epistemic boundary marker — read before trusting any "confirmed" below or in round 1

At ~20:00 local tonight this ledger's bar was raised: a "two sources agree" claim is
CONFIRMATION only when the two sources' **independent origin is provable** (typically by
timing); otherwise it is CORROBORATION-PENDING. Entries recorded before that hour — all of
round 1 and most of this round — were held to the round-1 standard (every claim carries
verified/not-verified-by-me limits) but NOT to provable-independence. "Confirmed" in
pre-bar entries means *verified against the artifact by this auditor*, not *independently
corroborated*. The three cases that revealed the difference: a reconciliation that closed
exactly on three cancelling wrong terms; a combined-green verdict masking an individual
red; and the consistency check that produced org rule 27. The danger is not that pre-bar
entries are wrong — it is that without this marker they would be indistinguishable from
entries that passed the stronger test. (Credit: ORYN-BASORG, who also noted the marker is
the same species of fix as dating a mislabelled artifact rather than re-litigating it.)

One post-bar entry already meets the strong standard: the UWA live-count agreement
(CFO query provably ran before BASORG's figure arrived; both instruments separately
built; both returned total 16,770 / UWA 107).

# The description field carries our own research notes to students — scoped

CEO's brief, following the walkthrough finding *"internal research-methodology notes render
verbatim to students... on 15+ of 24 cards."* That number was a byproduct of a walkthrough
sample, not a measurement — this scopes it properly: size it by hand-reading (not trusting a
regex), characterize how it clusters, and separate what gets deleted from what gets
rewritten. **Report only — no row written, no SQL applied.**

## A correction to the walkthrough's own 15/24, made before anything else

Rereading with `citizenship_restrictions`/`residency_restrictions` pulled alongside
`description` found something the walkthrough missed: **most of what looked like leaked
prose on the browse cards — "Country eligibility not verified yet," "Citizenship restriction
on file (not automatically verified)" — is not stored content at all.** It's generated by
`lib/opportunities/matching.ts`'s `eligibilityMessages`, which **already has real Turkish
strings for every one of these** (`"Ülke uygunluğu henüz doğrulanmadı..."`,
`"Kayıtlı vatandaşlık kısıtlaması (otomatik doğrulanmadı): ..."`). The function's own header
comment says this was found and fixed once already — *"measured 2026-09-01, 325 of 623
currently 'strong match' opportunities carry a note here... a Turkish student was seeing
English on roughly a third of their best recommendations"* — and it clearly regressed or
was never fully wired end to end, since it rendered in English again live tonight. **This is
a locale-propagation bug, not a description-content problem**, it's a different size
(potentially hundreds of match-explanation renders, not ~70 rows) and a different, much
smaller fix (thread `locale` through the missing call site) than anything below. Naming it
here so it doesn't get folded into the count that follows, and so it doesn't get lost either
— worth its own ticket.

## The real number, measured by reading, not by trusting a regex

**Net (18 phrases built from confirmed true positives — "as of this research," "fetched
page," "vendor claim," "measurement #," "official site confirmed," etc.) against all 366
active descriptions: 31 hits, all 31 confirmed genuine on a full read — zero false
positives.** High precision. Then checked what the net misses: read two independent random
samples of the 335 non-candidate rows (25, then 30 — 55 total, none cherry-picked) in full.
**Found 7 more genuine leaks the phrase net didn't catch** — different wording each time
(XLAB's *"cycle_status set to closed for this reason"*, IE JAB's *"Stored as 0, not NULL,
since those mean different things,"* Oxford Royale's *"not tracked by this column,"* Gates
Scholarship's *"tier is set to competitive_award,"* Penn Apps' *"pending a reviewer check,"*
Conrad Challenge's *"verify directly before treating Oct 29 as certain,"* and a milder
International Brain Bee instance). **7 of 55 is a 12.7% miss rate**, consistent across both
independent samples (3/25, then 4/30) rather than a one-off.

**Extrapolating that rate across the 335 non-candidate rows: roughly 43 more, for a total
estimate of ~74 rows — about 1 in 5 of the 366-row active catalogue.** Stated as an estimate
because it is one: two ~25-30 row samples projected onto a 335-row population, not a census.
The 31 hand-confirmed rows are exact; the ~74 is the honest reading of what a full hand-read
would very likely find, and matches this fleet's own repeated finding tonight that a keyword
net undercounts real language variation.

## What clusters, and how — checked against `created_at`/`updated_at`, not assumed

**Not one bulk import with one missing step, unlike the deadline and eligibility gaps.**
`created_at` for every active row stops at 2026-08-24 01:00 — the leak text is almost
entirely dated 08-24 or later in its own prose, meaning it was written by later `UPDATE`
passes, not the original imports. Checking `updated_at` instead shows the real shape: **many
small clusters, each a few minutes wide, spread across at least six different dates** (08-20,
08-21, 08-23, 08-24, 08-27, 08-31, 09-03) — one genuinely exact-microsecond cluster of 4 rows
sharing `2026-09-03 06:28:54.381135+00` (STEM Racing, Lumiere Education, The Wall Street 101
Summer Pre-College Program, Sevenoaks — one real `UPDATE` statement touching four rows), and
several 2-5 minute clusters elsewhere (the 08-31 18:10-18:12 window alone: IE University, Wall
Street 101 Virtual, UWC, Oxbridge, Global Achievers — five rows in under two minutes). **This
is a recurring authorial habit, not a single event**: whoever does this kind of verification
pass — and it looks like more than one person or session, given the pattern recurs across a
week and a half — tends to write their own reasoning about *how* a fact was confirmed
directly into the field meant to hold the fact itself, then moves to the next few rows and
does it again. One migration will not close this; it will recur the next time someone does
this kind of pass unless the habit itself changes.

**One template within the pattern genuinely is a single repeated event, not a habit**:
*"Program identity and official site confirmed; the fetched page [outcome]."* Appears
verbatim, word for word, on Oxbridge Academic Programs, University of Notre Dame Pre-College,
and Sabancı University Summer School — different timestamps (08-31, 08-31, 09-03) but
identical phrasing, meaning it's a copy-pasted boilerplate line reused by name across several
sessions specifically for the "page didn't confirm dates" case — the one part of this defect
that behaves like a fixable pattern rather than 70 individual edits.

## Delete versus rewrite — the ratio, from the 38 rows actually read in full

**Overwhelmingly rewrite, not delete: roughly 35 of the 38 confirmed leak rows have a real,
useful fact sitting inside the internal wrapping.** Pioneer Research Institute's credit claim
really was upgraded to a stronger source; InvestIN really isn't free and really does have a
scholarship scheme; Sevenoaks' GBP 5,940/4,675 figures are real and were verified twice.
**Only the three-row "Program identity confirmed" template is close to pure delete** — it
carries no fact beyond "dates unknown," which a student needs to know but which needs zero
research language to say.

**One row is worth naming as the far end of the spectrum**: IE University Pre-University
Summer Program's description ends in a full paragraph of unresolved internal flagging —
*"FLAG 2026-08-24, not yet resolved: this row is stored as `selectivity_tier='open_enrollment'`...
ie.edu redirect-looped on 3/3 direct-fetch attempts... `selectivity_evidence` has no column,
a known schema gap"* — two raw column names, a live disagreement about the row's own
correctness, and zero of it is a sentence a student could use. This is real editorial work,
not a find-and-replace: someone has to read each of the ~74, decide what fact (if any)
survives, and write one clean sentence for it. That is the ratio this brief asked for, and it
means this is a rewrite project sized in hours per researcher-week, not a single afternoon.

**No SQL staged, on purpose.** The three-row template could be mechanically blanked, but
almost everything else needs a person to read the wrapped fact and choose the sentence that
replaces it — writing 70-odd replacement descriptions is the fix itself, not a measurement of
it, and attempting it here would be doing the thing this brief explicitly asked not to do.

## Everything else from the walkthrough that wasn't this task, said now rather than later

Three things noticed live and set aside at the time because they weren't the walkthrough's
own scope — put here per CEO's instruction rather than left for someone to rediscover:

- **The opportunities filter header and its own results count disagree.** `/opportunities?view=browse`
  showed *"Tümü · 360"* as the category-chip total and *"366 fırsat eşleşiyor"* three lines
  below it, on the same page, same load. Not chased — could be a stale count on one side, a
  different underlying query, or a legitimate scope difference (e.g. one counts something the
  other excludes) — but two numbers claiming to answer "how many opportunities" on one screen
  should agree, and didn't.
- **A visible layout flash on cold navigation.** Navigating directly to `/universities` and
  `/plan` (typed URL, not a client-side link click) rendered a compact, mobile-shaped nav list
  ("Ana Sayfa / Ana Sayfa" duplicated, a "Keşfet" bottom-nav-style grouping) for one frame
  before the real desktop sidebar took over. Never reproduced on a client-side navigation,
  only a fresh load — reads like an SSR/hydration mismatch on the nav component, not chased
  further since it self-corrected every time.
- **The opportunities "best next step" pick, for this profile, was UCSB Research Mentorship
  Programs** — a program whose own description states 2026 dates had already elapsed and 2027
  dates weren't yet published, i.e. not currently applicable. The ranker diagnosis CEO
  mentioned as already closed and merged (`ab`, `994ed8b8`) is almost certainly the same root
  cause — noted here only as a live, independently-observed instance for whoever reads that
  diagnosis, not as a reopened investigation.

## What this pass did not do

Did not hand-read all 335 non-candidate rows — the ~74 figure is a two-sample estimate, named
as one. Did not classify all ~74 individually into delete/rewrite — the 92/8 ratio comes from
the 38 rows actually read in full, not the full estimated population. Did not touch the
locale-propagation bug, the currency defect, or any of the three "noticed in passing" items
above — all reported, none fixed, none staged.

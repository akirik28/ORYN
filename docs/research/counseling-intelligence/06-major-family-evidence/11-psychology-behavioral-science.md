# Major-Family Evidence: Psychology & Behavioral Science

Scope: psychology (clinical, social, cognitive, developmental), behavioral science, neuroscience
interest at the exploratory (non-lab-based-neuroscience-research) level.

## The crowded-major problem, named explicitly by the field's own professional body

Psychology is consistently one of the most popular intended majors among selective-university
applicants — meaning admissions readers see very high volumes of "interest in human behavior"
statements. The American Psychological Association runs its own structured pathways for
demonstrating *genuine* engagement rather than stated interest alone: an **APA High School Summer
Internship Program** placing students directly with professional psychologists for hands-on
research, alongside APA-run undergraduate research fellowships for the next stage up. (Source:
`apa.org` official program pages — high confidence, official professional body.)
[[RULE-COUNSEL-035]] The evidentiary distinction that matters most, stated plainly by
admissions-consulting sources describing this specific major: **"a summer program that ends with a
certificate proves attendance; a program that ends with a peer-reviewed published paper proves
original thinking"** — an unusually direct restatement of this package's evidence-state ladder
(`02-opportunity-development-mapping.md`) applied to one specific field. [[RULE-COUNSEL-036]]

## Mapping onto the shipped taxonomy

- `research` — the dominant dimension for this family; both exposure (structured program) and
  output (write-up, poster, publication) tiers apply exactly as `02`'s ladder describes.
- `intellectual_curiosity` — psychology's breadth (clinical/social/cognitive/developmental/
  neuroscience) rewards a student who has sampled sub-fields before committing, not a student who
  states "I love psychology" without differentiating which part.
- `community_impact` — for students drawn to the clinical/counseling-adjacent side specifically
  (peer support programs, mental-health awareness initiatives) — a legitimate, distinct evidence
  channel from lab-style research.

## Unsafe inferences specific to this family

- **Do not treat "interest in psychology" alone as meaningful signal** — given the field's
  popularity, stated interest is close to the least differentiating evidence ORYN could recommend
  around; the framework should push toward *which* sub-field and *what specific engagement*, not
  validate the stated interest as sufficient on its own. Directly consistent with
  `01-development-taxonomy.md`'s "stated interest is a starting hypothesis, not development
  evidence" finding.
- **Do not require lab access for legitimate research evidence** — much of psychology (survey
  design, observational studies, literature review/synthesis, even a well-designed personal
  experiment on a permitted topic) is genuinely accessible without institutional lab access, unlike
  bench-science fields. This is a real access-equity distinction worth naming for gap-framework
  purposes (`04-profile-gap-framework.md`): a "no research" gap in psychology specifically has a
  lower legitimate access excuse than the same gap in, say, biomedical research requiring wet-lab
  equipment. [[RULE-COUNSEL-037]]

## Rules established in this document

- `RULE-COUNSEL-035` — Recognize APA's own structured high-school and undergraduate research
  pathways as a credible, official evidence channel for this family. Confidence: high (official
  source).
- `RULE-COUNSEL-036` — Apply the achievement-tier ladder explicitly for psychology-track research:
  attendance/certificate is a real but lower tier than a produced, presented, or published output.
  Confidence: high.
- `RULE-COUNSEL-037` — Do not extend the "research requires institutional access" caution
  uniformly across all fields — psychology-track research has a meaningfully lower access barrier
  than lab/bench-science research, and gap-severity reasoning should reflect that difference rather
  than a single blanket rule. Confidence: medium (reasoned distinction, not independently
  empirically tested by this package).
- `RULE-COUNSEL-084` — İŞKUR's official Future Occupations list includes Psikolog (Psychologist)
  and Özel Eğitim Öğretmeni (Special Education Teacher, psychology-adjacent), confirming domestic
  Turkish labor-market relevance for career-exploration framing only. Confidence: high (official
  source, for the list's content).

# Major-Family Evidence: Sociology, History & Philosophy

Scope: sociology, history, philosophy, and adjacent social-theory/ethics interest — grouped
because they share an evidence profile (original argument/research built from primary sources or
structured reasoning) more than they share subject content.

## An official, structured, high-school-native evidence channel exists for history specifically

**National History Day** is a long-running, official, nationwide contest structured almost exactly
like a research-methods on-ramp: students produce **original historical research** presented
through a choice of format (documentary, performance, exhibit, website, or traditional paper),
explicitly developing "historical research, communication, project management, and critical
thinking," with a formal judged-feedback-and-revision cycle between contest levels. (Source:
`nhd.org`, official contest site — high confidence.) [[RULE-COUNSEL-044]] This is a genuinely
strong example of the achievement-tier ladder (`02-opportunity-development-mapping.md`) applying
cleanly within one family: `participated` (entered a project) → `advanced` (qualified past
school/regional rounds) → `placed` (state/national medal) are all real, distinguishable,
officially-tracked tiers for one activity.

**Philosophy has a comparable, if smaller, structured channel**: the **National High School Ethics
Bowl**, a competitive-but-collaborative format (teams are judged on depth of ethical reasoning
about real cases, not adversarial "winning" the way debate is) — a legitimate, official evidence
source for philosophical/ethical reasoning specifically. (Source: `nhseb.org`, official contest
site — high confidence for the program's existence and format.) [[RULE-COUNSEL-045]]

**Sociology has no equivalent single flagship high-school contest** this package found — its
natural evidence channel is closer to `psychology`'s (survey/observational research, community-
based studies) than to a competition format, and should draw on
`11-psychology-behavioral-science.md`'s access-aware research framing (community-based research is
genuinely accessible without institutional lab access) rather than assuming a missing "Sociology
Day"-equivalent means the student lacks options.

## Mapping onto the shipped taxonomy

- `research` — primary-source historical research, sociological studies, philosophical argument
  papers; process-over-output applies fully (a well-argued unpublished essay is real evidence).
- `execution_project_depth` — a produced NHD-style project (documentary, exhibit) beyond a written
  paper alone — this family has an unusually strong claim on evidence that spans both `research`
  and `execution_project_depth` simultaneously, per `01`'s "one activity, multiple dimensions"
  principle.
- `intellectual_curiosity` — independent reading/engagement beyond assigned coursework, a natural
  fit for philosophy/history specifically.

## Unsafe inferences specific to this family

- **Do not assume history/philosophy interest requires a competition result to be legitimate
  evidence** — sustained independent reading, a well-developed personal essay/argument, or a
  self-directed research project are real evidence even with zero competitive validation, per
  `02`'s "publication/output is a bonus tier, not a requirement" principle applied here.
- **Do not conflate debate-format skill (adversarial, per `12`/`13`) with ethics-bowl-format skill
  (collaborative, depth-of-reasoning judged)** — these test meaningfully different things despite
  surface similarity; an explanation citing Ethics Bowl participation should describe reasoning
  depth, not "won an argument."

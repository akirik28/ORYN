# Foreign-Language Exams in Turkey

Researched 2026-08-21 from ÖSYM primary sources: the **Uluslararası Yabancı Dil Sınavları Eşdeğerlikleri Dokümanı** (updated 11.03.2025) and the **2026-YÖKDİL/1 Kılavuzu** (32pp).

**Headline for ORYN: with one exception, none of the exams in this document are relevant to a 14–18 year-old.** YDS, e-YDS and YÖKDİL are postgraduate, academic-career and civil-service instruments. The exams that actually matter to our users are **YDT** (inside YKS, see `02-yks-and-obp.md`), **UDSP** (see `01-udsp-identification.md`), and international exams taken for applications abroad.

This document exists mainly so ORYN can correctly *rule these out* and answer "should I take YDS?" with a confident, evidence-backed **no** for a secondary student.

---

## 1. The ÜDS question — settled by ÖSYM's own document — `RULE-TR-EXAM-020`

The task brief hypothesised that "UDSP" might be a garbled **ÜDS** (Üniversitelerarası Kurul Yabancı Dil Sınavı), "believed abolished/replaced by YDS around 2013 — verify this". UDSP turned out to be a real and separate exam (`01-udsp-identification.md`), but the ÜDS question deserves a direct answer.

ÖSYM's current equivalence document states, verbatim:

> "**KPDS/ÜDS/YDS/e-YDS Eşdeğerliği**
> ÖSYM tarafından yapılan, Kamu Personeli Yabancı Dil Bilgisi Seviye Tespit Sınavı (KPDS), Üniversitelerarası Kurul Yabancı Dil Sınavı (ÜDS), Yabancı Dil Bilgisi Seviye Tespit Sınavı (YDS) ve Elektronik Yabancı Dil Bilgisi Seviye Tespit Sınavı (e-YDS) birbirlerine **çift yönlü olarak denktir/eşdeğerdir**."

What this establishes, on ÖSYM's own authority:

- **ÜDS was real** — full name Üniversitelerarası Kurul Yabancı Dil Sınavı, administered by ÖSYM.
- **ÜDS, KPDS, YDS and e-YDS are mutually (bidirectionally) equivalent.** An old ÜDS certificate still converts to a YDS-equivalent score today; ÖSYM continues to document the mapping as of March 2025.
- **ÖSYM no longer administers ÜDS or KPDS.** Its current foreign-language offering is YDS, e-YDS, YÖKDİL and e-YÖKDİL. ÜDS and KPDS appear only in the equivalence table — as historical scores to be honoured, not as exams to sit.

### What I could NOT verify

The commonly-repeated claim that **ÜDS and KPDS were formally abolished and merged into YDS in 2013** by the *Yabancı Dil Bilgisi Seviye Belirleme Usul ve Esasları Hakkında Yönetmelik* (Resmî Gazete 4/1/2013, No. 28518) is **plausible and consistent with the evidence above, but I could not read the regulation's text.** Both `resmigazete.gov.tr` and `mevzuat.gov.tr` failed TLS certificate verification from this environment on repeated attempts.

The regulation's existence, date and gazette number were returned by a search restricted to those two official domains, so the citation itself is sound. The specific **repeal article** naming ÜDS/KPDS is **not** verified.

- Status: **the exams' equivalence and ÖSYM's current catalogue are `VERIFIED_CURRENT`**; the *2013 abolition mechanism* is `NEEDS_REVIEW`.
- ORYN should say "ÖSYM no longer administers ÜDS; its scores remain equivalent to YDS" — a claim fully supported — rather than "ÜDS was abolished in 2013", which is not yet sourced to primary text.

## 2. The ÖSYM exam family

| Exam | Full name | Administered by | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **YDS** | Yabancı Dil Bilgisi Seviye Tespit Sınavı | ÖSYM | Civil servants, academics, postgraduate applicants |
| **e-YDS** | Elektronik YDS | ÖSYM | Same, computer-based, more frequent sittings |
| **YÖKDİL** | Yükseköğretim Kurumları Yabancı Dil Sınavı | ÖSYM under protocol with **YÖK** | Higher-education staff and postgraduate applicants |
| **e-YÖKDİL** | Electronic YÖKDİL | ÖSYM | Same |
| **ÜDS / KPDS** | *(historical)* | ÖSYM | **No longer administered** — legacy scores only |

All are scored out of **100**.

### YÖKDİL specifics — `RULE-TR-EXAM-021`

From the 2026-YÖKDİL/1 kılavuz:

> "Yükseköğretim Kurumları Yabancı Dil Sınavı (YÖKDİL), Yükseköğretim Kurulu Başkanlığı (YÖK) ile Ölçme, Seçme ve Yerleştirme Merkezi Başkanlığı (ÖSYM) arasında imzalanan protokol uyarınca, ÖSYM tarafından yapılacaktır."

- **Discipline-specific**, unlike YDS: streams in **Fen Bilimleri, Sağlık Bilimleri, Sosyal Bilimler**.
- Held **twice a year**. Spring: English in all three fields, Arabic in Social Sciences only. Autumn: English in all three fields, plus German, Arabic and French in Social Sciences only.
- 2026 dates: **YÖKDİL/1 on 8 March 2026**; **YÖKDİL/2 on 2 August 2026**. Both start 10:15 and run **180 minutes**.
- One language and one field per session only.
- **Validity is split** — and this is unusual enough to be worth recording precisely:

> "Sınav sonuçları, lisansüstü eğitim ve yabancı dil hazırlık muafiyetinde sınavın gerçekleştirildiği tarihten itibaren **5 (beş) yıl**, öğretim elemanı kadroları ile doçentlik başvurularında ise **sürekli** geçerlidir."

Five years for postgraduate study and prep-year exemption; **permanently valid** for academic-staff posts and associate-professorship (doçentlik) applications.

**Every use case listed is postgraduate or professional.** There is no secondary-school application of YÖKDİL.

## 3. International exams — how they map to YDS — `RULE-TR-EXAM-022`

### The critical finding: IELTS is not on the list

ÖSYM's equivalence document names the accepted **English** exams explicitly:

> "İngilizce için eşdeğerliği kabul edilen **Cambridge C2 Proficiency, Cambridge C1 Advanced, Cambridge Linguaskill General, TOEFL iBT, PTE Academic, Oxford Test Of English Advanced ve Oxford Test Of English** sınavlarının eşdeğer olduğu KPDS/ÜDS/YDS/e-YDS puanları Tablo 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 ve 7'de verilmiştir."

**IELTS does not appear anywhere in the document** — zero occurrences across all six pages, in the prose or any table. The document is explicit that this list is closed:

> "Dokümanda belirtilenlerin dışında kalan yabancı dil sınavları bu eşdeğerlik dokümanına göre değerlendirilemez."

**What this does and does not mean.** It means an IELTS score **cannot be converted into a YDS-equivalent score** for the purposes ÖSYM equivalence governs — postgraduate admission, academic posts, civil-service language allowance. It does **not** mean IELTS is unusable in Turkey: individual universities set their own English-proficiency and prep-year-exemption rules under their own senate regulations, and many accept IELTS directly. **I did not verify any individual university's policy**, and ORYN must not generalise in either direction. Status of the broader "is IELTS accepted in Turkey" question: `UNVERIFIED` — it is per-institution, and must be sourced per institution.

### Rules that constrain equivalence

Four provisions ORYN should surface whenever it touches this area:

1. **Equivalence runs one way.** "Uluslararası yabancı dil sınavları… KPDS, ÜDS, YDS veya e-YDS ile **tek yönlü** olarak eşdeğerdir." TOEFL converts *to* a YDS score; a YDS score does not convert back into a TOEFL score. ORYN must never present a YDS→TOEFL conversion.
2. **Five-year default validity.** If the certificate carries no expiry, the score is usable for equivalence for **5 years from the exam date**.
3. **Venue and documentation rules.** Sessions held in Turkey must take place **in state university buildings**, and result documents must show exam **place and date** — otherwise the result is not accepted as equivalent.
4. **The document version is pinned to the exam date.** Equivalence is assessed against the document in force **on the day the exam was taken**, not the day of application (with a 30-day transition for applications already filed). ÖSYM publishes historical versions back to 6 January 2012. **ORYN must therefore never treat the current table as retroactively applicable to an older certificate.**

Finally: "Başkanlığımızca eşdeğerlik belgesi yerine geçecek herhangi bir belge verilmez" — **ÖSYM issues no equivalence certificate.** Institutions apply the table themselves. A student cannot obtain a document from ÖSYM proving their TOEFL equals a given YDS score.

## 4. Freshness

The equivalence document in force at retrieval was dated **11.03.2025** — roughly 17 months old as of 2026-08-21. ÖSYM updates it irregularly and has removed exams from the list in the past (the document anticipates "eşdeğerliği kaldırılmış sınavlar"). **ORYN must re-check this document rather than caching the tables above indefinitely**, and should display the document's update date alongside any conversion it shows.

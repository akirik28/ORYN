# YKS and OBP — Turkey's University Entrance System

Researched 2026-08-21. Every fact below is from ÖSYM primary documents: the **2026-YKS Kılavuzu** (146pp), the **2026-YKS Yükseköğretim Programları ve Kontenjanları Kılavuzu** (793pp), and **2026-YKS Sayısal Bilgiler** (official post-exam statistics). No secondary source is used as evidence anywhere in this document.

The 2026 cycle has completed (exams 20–21 June 2026; placement guide published 21 July 2026), so 2026 facts are `VERIFIED_HISTORICAL`. **2027 details are not published** — see the freshness warning at the end.

---

## 1. Structure

YKS (Yükseköğretim Kurumları Sınavı) is run by **ÖSYM** and consists of three sessions over one weekend:

| Session | Test | 2026 date | Start | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **TYT** — Temel Yeterlilik Testi | Sat 20 June 2026 | 10:15 | **165 min** |
| 2 | **AYT** — Alan Yeterlilik Testleri | Sun 21 June 2026 | 10:15 | **180 min** |
| 3 | **YDT** — Yabancı Dil Testi | Sun 21 June 2026 | 15:45 | **120 min** |

Applications: 6 Feb – 2 Mar 2026 (payment by 3 Mar). Late window 10–12 Mar at +50%. Fee **700 TL per session**.

**TYT is compulsory for all candidates** ("YKS'nin 1. Oturumu olan Temel Yeterlilik Testi (TYT), tüm adaylar için zorunludur"). AYT and YDT are opt-in.

### Question counts (Tablo 1B)

**TYT — 120 questions:** Türkçe 40 · Sosyal Bilimler 20 (Tarih 5, Coğrafya 5, Felsefe 5, DKAB-or-extra-Felsefe 5) · Temel Matematik 40 · Fen Bilimleri 20 (Fizik 7, Kimya 7, Biyoloji 6)

**AYT — one booklet, candidate answers only what their target score type needs:**
- Türk Dili ve Edebiyatı–Sosyal Bilimler-1: TDE 24, Tarih-1 10, Coğrafya-1 6
- Sosyal Bilimler-2: Tarih-2 11, Coğrafya-2 11, Felsefe Grubu 12, DKAB-or-extra 6
- Matematik: 40
- Fen Bilimleri: Fizik 14, Kimya 13, Biyoloji 13

**YDT — 80 questions**, in German, Arabic, French, English or Russian (separate booklet per language, fixed at application).

> Candidates are examined against a MEB-published syllabus, not an ÖSYM-invented one: *"ÖSYM tarafından 2026 Yılında Gerçekleştirilecek TYT, AYT ve YDT Sınavlarına Esas Ortak Derslere Ait Kazanım ve Açıklamalar"*, at `https://ttkb.meb.gov.tr/meb_iys_dosyalar/2025_11/26164023_2026_yks.pdf`. This is the authoritative scope document — ORYN should point students at it rather than at any prep-company topic list.

### The DKAB substitution — `RULE-TR-EXAM-008`

Students who were not required to take Din Kültürü ve Ahlak Bilgisi, or took a different curriculum (**excluding İmam Hatip students/graduates**), answer extra Philosophy questions instead. This matters for ORYN's international-curriculum users, who frequently fall into this category:

- **TYT Sosyal Bilimler** carries 25 answer slots. All candidates answer Q1–15. DKAB-takers then do Q16–20; non-takers skip those and do Q21–25 (Felsefe) instead.
- **AYT Sosyal Bilimler-2** carries 46 slots. All answer Q1–34. DKAB-takers do Q35–40; non-takers do Q41–46 (Felsefe 2, Psikoloji 1, Sosyoloji 2, Mantık 1).

The choice is declared **on the application form** (field 25), not on exam day. Marks made in the wrong block are discarded outright. A student who mis-declares in February loses those questions in June with no remedy — worth surfacing as a hard deadline-linked check.

## 2. Scoring — `RULE-TR-EXAM-009`

### Raw score: wrong answers are penalised

> "Doğru cevap sayısından yanlış cevap sayısının **dörtte biri** çıkarılarak adayın ilgili testten almış olduğu ham puan bulunacaktır." — Kılavuz 3.10.1

**Raw (net) = correct − (wrong ÷ 4).** This is the exact opposite of UDSP, which counts only correct answers (`RULE-TR-EXAM-006`). ORYN must never give generic "always guess" advice across Turkish exams — the correct strategy is exam-specific. On YKS, a blind 5-way guess is EV-neutral at best; eliminating one option makes it positive.

### Standardisation — scores are relative, not absolute

Raw scores are converted to standard scores with **mean 50, SD 10**. Critically:

> "**Son sınıfta okumakta olan** tüm adayların ilgili testten aldıkları ham puanlar kullanılarak o testin ortalama ve standart sapması bulunacaktır."

The norm group is **final-year students only**, though standard scores are then computed for everyone (including repeat candidates and graduates). Two consequences ORYN must respect:

1. **"How many nets do I need for X?" has no stable answer.** The mapping from nets to score to rank shifts every year with the cohort and paper difficulty. Any ORYN feature quoting a net→rank conversion must be labelled with its source year and must not be presented as a forecast for the student's own cycle.
2. It is a **rank tournament**, not a criterion test. A student improves their position only by outperforming the cohort.

Weighted scores (A-TYT, A-SAY, A-SÖZ, A-EA, A-DİL) are each rescaled to a **100–500** range.

### Weights — where effort actually lands — `RULE-TR-EXAM-010`

**Tablo 1C — TYT composite:**

| Türkçe | Sosyal Bilimler | Temel Matematik | Fen Bilimleri |
|---|---|---|---|
| **33%** | 17% | **33%** | 17% |

**Tablo 1E — the four AYT/YDT score types.** Note the first column:

| Type | **TYT** | AYT/YDT components |
|---|---|---|
| **SAY** | **40%** | Matematik 30 · Fizik 10 · Kimya 10 · Biyoloji 10 |
| **EA** | **40%** | Matematik 30 · TDE 18 · Tarih-1 7 · Coğrafya-1 5 |
| **SÖZ** | **40%** | TDE 18 · Tarih-1 7 · Coğrafya-1 5 · Tarih-2 8 · Coğrafya-2 8 · Felsefe Grubu 9 · DKAB/İlave 5 |
| **DİL** | **40%** | Yabancı Dil Testi 60 |

**This is the single most important structural fact in Turkish university admissions, and it is widely underweighted by students:** TYT is 40% of *every* score type. Combined with Tablo 1C, TYT Türkçe alone is 0.40 × 0.33 = **13.2% of a medical applicant's SAY score** — more than Physics, Chemistry or Biology individually (10% each). Temel Matematik is another 13.2%.

A student targeting medicine who neglects TYT Türkçe to grind AYT Fizik has the trade-off backwards.

### Minimum conditions to get a score at all — `RULE-TR-EXAM-011`

Tablo 1D. A weighted score is computed **only if** the candidate clears a raw-score floor of **0.5**:

- **TYT**: ≥0.5 raw in Türkçe **or** Temel Matematik
- **SAY**: the TYT condition **and** ≥0.5 raw in AYT Matematik **or** Fen Bilimleri
- **EA**: TYT condition **and** ≥0.5 in AYT Matematik **or** TDE-Sosyal-1
- **SÖZ**: TYT condition **and** ≥0.5 in TDE-Sosyal-1 **or** Sosyal Bilimler-2
- **DİL**: TYT condition **and** ≥0.5 raw in the YDT

And the hard dependency:

> "TYT puanı olmayan, TYT puanı hesaplanmayan adayların, AYT ve/veya YDT'ye girmiş olsalar da SAY, SÖZ, EA, DİL puanları **hesaplanmayacaktır**."

**Failing to produce a TYT score voids the entire cycle**, no matter how well the student did on AYT. The floor is very low (0.5 net ≈ one correct answer net of penalties), but it is absolute.

### There is no longer a numeric "baraj" — `RULE-TR-EXAM-012`

This is a live correction to widely-repeated folklore. The 2026 kılavuz contains **no** 150-point or 180-point threshold — the word *baraj* does not appear in the document at all. Tablo 1F requires only that the relevant score **has been calculated**. Instead, specific fields are gated by **başarı sırası (success rank)**, per Tablo 1G:

| Field | Score type | Minimum rank |
|---|---|---|
| **Tıp** (medicine) | SAY | 50,000 |
| **Diş Hekimliği** | SAY | 80,000 |
| **Eczacılık** | SAY | 100,000 |
| **Hukuk** (law) | EA | 100,000 |
| **Mimarlık** (architecture) | SAY | 250,000 |
| **Mühendislik** (engineering)¹ | SAY | 300,000 |
| **Öğretmenlik** (teaching, incl. PDR) | relevant type | 300,000 |
| Teaching programs via ÖZYES | TYT | 800,000² |

¹ Excludes Forestry/Agriculture/Fisheries-faculty programs and several named ones; *includes* Food Engineering in Agriculture faculties.
² Uses the **rank of the placement score without extra points** (Y-TYT).

> **Foundation (vakıf) universities may set a *stricter* rank than these** by senate decision: "Vakıf Yükseköğretim kurumları senato kararı ile söz konusu başarı sırasına ilişkin daha üstte başarı sırası belirleyebilmektedir." ORYN must therefore never present the Tablo 1G rank as sufficient for a specific private-university program — it is a national floor, not a program requirement.

### Cancelled questions are re-weighted, not zeroed

Cancelled questions are excluded and remaining questions re-valued. This is not theoretical: comparing Tablo 1B against the official 2026 Sayısal Bilgiler shows **AYT Matematik was scored over 39 of 40 questions and AYT Türk Dili ve Edebiyatı over 23 of 24** in 2026. Any ORYN logic assuming a fixed denominator per test will be wrong in most years.

Two observations ORYN can legitimately draw from this official data:

- **Mathematics is where the distribution is thinnest.** A mean of 6.4/40 on TYT Temel Matematik and 7.1/39 on AYT Matematik, against SDs of 7.5 and 8.9, means most candidates are clustered near the floor. Because scores are standardised on SD, marginal net gains in maths move rank disproportionately compared to the same gains in Türkçe (mean 20.1, SD 8.3, a much more crowded middle).
- **YDT is a small, self-selected pool.** 143k valid vs 2.25m on TYT. The English mean of 37.6/80 is *not* evidence that YDT is easy — it is a pool that already opted into language study.

> ORYN must present these as **2026 cohort statistics**, never as predictions or as "the score you need". They describe what happened, not what will happen.

## 4. OBP — how school grades enter the score

### Calculation — `RULE-TR-EXAM-013`

> "Ortaöğretimde alınan 100 üzerinden diploma notu, **5 ile çarpılarak** Ortaöğretim Başarı Puanına (OBP) dönüştürülecektir. Böylece, 50 olan en düşük diploma notu için OBP 250 olacak, en yüksek 100 olan diploma notu için de OBP 500 olacaktır." — Kılavuz 4.1

- **OBP = diploma grade (out of 100) × 5**, giving a **250–500** range.
- **Diploma grades below 50, and unreported grades, are treated as 50** — so OBP has a hard floor of 250 and can never be zero.
- **No school-level adjustment whatsoever:** "Ortaöğretim Başarı Puanının (OBP) hesaplanmasında **Türkiye geneli dikkate alınacak, okul bazında işlem yapılmayacaktır**." A 90 average from a highly selective science high school and a 90 from a non-selective school produce the identical OBP. ORYN must not model any school-quality correction — the regulation explicitly forbids one.

The diploma grade is pulled from **MEB e-Okul** into ÖSYM's system in June. Corrections go **through the school on e-Okul, never through ÖSYM**, and candidates must verify and approve their education record in AİS between **23–30 June 2026, by 16:00**. ÖSYM disclaims responsibility for uncorrected errors. This is a real, dated, actionable checkpoint — exactly the kind of item ORYN's deadline engine should carry.

### Contribution to placement score — `RULE-TR-EXAM-014`

> "Her aday için hesaplanmış olan OBP; **0,12 katsayısı** ile çarpılarak sınav puanlarına eklenecek…"

**Placement score = exam score + (OBP × 0.12)**, so the contribution ranges from **30 points (OBP 250) to 60 points (OBP 500)** — a **30-point spread** on a 100–500 scale.

Vocational-track graduates additionally receive **OBP × 0.06** as *ek puan* when placing into related programs listed in Tablo 3A/3B.1/3C/3D. For Tablo 3A and 3B.1 this extra point applies **only to students enrolled at such a school on or before 30.03.2012** — i.e. it is a legacy provision that no current 14–18 student can qualify for. ORYN should treat 3A/3B.1 ek puan as dead for its user base while noting 3C/3D carry no such date restriction in the text quoted.

### The repeat-candidate penalty — `RULE-TR-EXAM-015`

This is the highest-stakes, most misunderstood rule in the system, and the place where ORYN can prevent real harm.

> "2025-YKS puanları ile bir yükseköğretim programına ÖSYM tarafından merkezî olarak (ÖZYES dâhil) **yerleştirilen (kayıt olsun veya olmasın)** veya ilgili yükseköğretim kurumu tarafından yapılan özel yetenek sınavı sonucu bir programa **kayıt olan** adaylar, 2026-YKS puanları ile bir yükseköğretim programına yerleştirilirken OBP'ye uygulanacak katsayılar **yarıya düşürülecektir**."

Read the parenthetical carefully. Two different triggers:

- **Central placement (ÖSYM), including ÖZYES: being *placed* is enough — "whether or not they register."** Declining the seat, never enrolling, and never setting foot on campus does **not** protect the student.
- **Özel yetenek (talent-exam) admission: the trigger is actually *registering*.**

Effect: the coefficient drops **0.12 → 0.06** (and the vocational ek puan 0.06 → 0.03). For a strong student with OBP 500, the OBP contribution falls from 60 to 30 — **a 30-point loss** carried into a rank tournament where a handful of points can span thousands of places.

**The practical counseling consequence is sharp and time-bound:** a student who is unsure whether they will accept a place, and who is seriously considering re-sitting next year, is penalised by *listing and winning* a preference they don't want. The decision point is at **tercih (preference submission)**, not at registration — and by the time the student learns this, it is irreversible. ORYN should surface this before the preference deadline for any user flagged as a potential repeat candidate.

**The one documented exception:** candidates who were final-year students in 2025-YKS, were placed, but could not register because they had not graduated in time (resit/single-subject exam cases) do **not** have their coefficient halved. MEB e-Okul records are used automatically; no petition to ÖSYM is needed.

### Other OBP roles

- **Tie-breaking**: where placement scores tie, the candidate with the higher OBP wins; if still tied, the **younger** candidate (birth date closer to the present).
- **Özel yetenek (talent) placement formula**, where OBP appears twice:
  `YP = (0,55 × ÖYSP × 5) + (0,12 × OBP) + (0,45 × TYT-P) + (0,06 × OBP)` — the final `0,06 × OBP` term applies to candidates from the relevant vocational field; otherwise the formula ends at `(0,45 × TYT-P)`.
- **International science olympiad medallists** get OBP multiplied by an extra coefficient — **0.06 for 1st place, 0.05 for 2nd, 0.04 for 3rd** — when the relevant programs are among their preferences. This is one of the very few routes by which an extracurricular achievement directly changes a YKS placement score. See `06-counseling-implications.md`.

## 5. What a student controls, and what they don't

| Lever | Controllable? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| TYT performance | **Yes — highest leverage** | 40% of every score type; 33/33/17/17 internally |
| AYT subject nets | Yes | Weight depends entirely on target score type |
| Choice of score type | Yes, but early | Determines which AYT tests matter; effectively set by school track |
| DKAB/Felsefe declaration | Yes — **at application, February** | Irreversible after the form closes |
| Diploma grade → OBP | Yes, slowly | Accumulates over 4 years; max 30-point swing |
| Verifying diploma grade in AİS | **Yes — 8-day window** | 23–30 June, via school/e-Okul only |
| Avoiding the halved coefficient | **Yes — at tercih time** | See `RULE-TR-EXAM-015` |
| Cohort difficulty / standardisation | **No** | Rank is relative; nets→rank shifts yearly |
| School quality adjustment | **No — forbidden by regulation** | OBP is Turkey-wide, not school-based |
| Essays, references, interviews, portfolio | **Not part of the system at all** | See `06-counseling-implications.md` |

## Freshness warning

**2027 YKS details are not published as of 2026-08-21.** Dates, fees, question counts, weights and rank thresholds have all changed between cycles historically, and the 2026 fee (700 TL/session) is a nominal TRY amount in a high-inflation economy. ORYN must label all of the above as **2026-cycle** facts and must not carry any of them forward as 2027 values.

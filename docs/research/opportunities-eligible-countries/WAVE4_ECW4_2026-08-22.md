- **Garcia Summer Research Program** (ECW4-002): a genuinely new *shape* of
  compound/null-by-design finding for this programme — gated by **immigration/visa status**

## Method reference for the successor (so the line lands in the same place)

- **Forward-vs-backward framing test** (now an org-wide rule per BASORG): language naming
  who the program is FOR, in present/ongoing terms ("open to... any country," "welcomes...
  from around the world," "for... students... from all over the world") → confirmed-open.
  Language only describing past/current attendance composition ("students come from X
  countries," "N countries represented," "a global community of students from...") →
  no-statement-found, even when internationally flavored.
- **RATIFIED distinction**: a program naming non-domestic applicants as a **defined
  eligible category with their own stated requirements** (differentiated deadline, age
  floor, English-proficiency test, visa/guardian provision) → confirmed-open, because
  specifying HOW they apply presupposes THAT they can. A page merely *having* an
  "International Students" resources link or office, with no accompanying explicit clause
  → no-statement-found. Both classes appear side by side in this wave's records with the
  distinction stated in each one's own `notes` field, not just in this summary.
- **RULE-ATTRIBUTION-001**: a page may describe several distinct products/tracks (e.g. a
  residential program and a separate online program). Favorable language belongs to the
  product it actually describes, not to every product on the page it appears on. Checked
  explicitly for every multi-track institution this wave (Iowa Young Writers, JHU CTY,
  Duke's commuter-vs-residential note, etc.).
- **RULE-FETCH-001, all 4 shapes now observed in this lane's own work**: (1) `robots.txt
  Disallow: /` → policy block, defer, never route around (CyberPatriot, wave 3). (2)
  Tooling-level 403/failure with a clean site → try `browser_render`, record it honestly,
  not a deferral (used repeatedly this wave: UVA, JHU CTY, Koç, AwesomeMath, Idyllwild,
  Durham, ISSOS). (3) An active challenge-response defense the browser also hits → defer
  regardless of `robots.txt` (Juilliard, this wave). (4) This session's own tooling
  refusing a domain outright, independent of the target site's defenses (`nytimes.com`,
  wave 3) — not encountered again this wave but stays in the rule set.
- **All-but-one enumeration is fabrication**: an explicit EXCLUSION (e.g. a sanctions list,
  or Garcia's immigration-status gate this wave) must never be inverted into a flat
  "everyone except X" inclusion list. Prose proposal or compound/null-by-design instead.
- **Never populate from structural inference alone**: a "national" program, a title
  containing a country name, a curriculum-terminology hint (e.g. "Year 12/A-levels")
  without an explicit residency/citizenship clause — none of these alone support a
  populate. This wave held that line on BETA Camp, HSHSP, University of Bath's Step into
  Bath, and FRC Türkiye (wave 3), among others — each recorded as a real, useful
  near-signal in its own `notes`, not silently discarded and not acted on prematurely.
